Research

Tight Approximation for Public Event Scheduling with Busy Agents

Kaiyan Yin*, Gengqi Yuan*, Jiajun Zhang*, Ruilong Zhang*

AAAI 2027 · Under review · OpenReview

* Equal contribution.

We give a randomized (1−1/e−ε)-approximation for PESBA via continuous greedy on a partition-aware extension, prove a matching hardness reduction from Max-k-Coverage, and present a simpler exact algorithm for the one-event case.

Noise Robustness of GRPO Training for Vision-Language Models under Noisy Teacher Annotations

Aug. 2026 · Conducted during internship at Alibaba Group · Ongoing

SFT+GRPO for VLMs remains largely robust to random annotation noise under greedy decoding (~1pp drop up to 40%), but can reinforce learnable systematic errors, while sampling evaluation reveals much larger confidence degradation.
